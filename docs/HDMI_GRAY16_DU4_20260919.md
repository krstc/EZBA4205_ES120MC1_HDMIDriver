# ES120MC1 HDMI 16-level blue-noise + DU4 candidate

## Purpose

This candidate addresses two observed defects in the live HDMI image:

- the previous 16x16, 256-threshold binary halftone covered midtones with a
  fine random-looking texture, producing a gray frosted-glass appearance;
- three repeated DU scans left severe trails during scrolling and motion.

The known-good EDID, ADV7611 receiver setup, HDMI geometry, DDR layout and
ES120MC1 row/column timing are unchanged.

## Grayscale conversion

The HDMI path still expands studio-range luma and applies the existing
contrast curve. Pure black and pure white endpoint bands remain solid so that
document backgrounds and text edges do not acquire noise.

The usable middle range is explicitly quantized into fourteen levels. With
solid black and solid white this gives exactly sixteen stable output states.
Each intermediate state is rendered by a fixed 4x4 blue-noise rank cell:

```text
 4 11  8  1
 9  5  6 12
 3  2 13  0
14  7 15 10
```

The rank cell is fixed in panel coordinates. It never changes between frames,
so a static source does not create temporal dither or needless ink movement.

This is sixteen-level spatial grayscale on the fast binary DU path. It is not
the panel's true 30-phase GC16 waveform. A full ED047TC1 GC16 update needs 30
panel scans and would update at about 2.5 fps with the current 75 Hz scan rate.

## Ghosting compromise

Fast HDMI updates now repeat the EPDiy-compatible binary DU drive four times
instead of three. With the measured panel scan rate:

- one panel scan: 75.005696 Hz;
- HDMI DU4 update: 18.751424 fps;
- complete DU5 update: 15.001139 fps.

DU4 gives each changed pixel 33 percent more drive time than DU3. It should
reduce scrolling trails without introducing an automatic full-screen flash.
The existing M18/K3 manual white-clean and framebuffer rebase path is retained.

## Verification

- `es120_dither_tb`: checks all sixteen endpoint-protected gray states and
  exact 4x4-cell density over a 16x16 frame.
- `es120_waveform_tb`: checks power-on clean, DU5, DU4 and absence of an
  unintended fifth fast scan.
- `check_epdiy_reference.py`: checks all twenty binary MODE_DU LUT transitions
  against the EPDiy ED047TC1 waveform and reports the resulting frame rates.
